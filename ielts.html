Which IELTS skills can I practise here?
The Express demo currently provides split-screen Reading and Listening questions, two Academic Writing tasks, and a browser microphone Speaking practice flow for Parts 1–3. Writing/Speaking do not return AI band scores until a real evaluation service is connected.
Which tests are playable right now?
The original Express sample set includes playable Listening and Reading questions, Writing Task 1 and Task 2 prompts, Speaking Parts 1–3 recording, and an integrated Reading/Listening/Writing demo suite. Cambridge Academic and Actual Tests collections are placeholders until licensed question, image and audio assets are connected.
Is this the official IELTS software?
No. The interface shown on this page is an illustrative practice preview. It is intended to help you get familiar with a question-based layout and is not affiliated with or endorsed by IELTS, the British Council, IDP or Cambridge University Press.
Will I get an official band score from every test?
Do not treat a practice score as an official IELTS result. Scoring availability and reliability depend on the specific test and connected scoring implementation; check the result screen for what is actually evaluated.
How should I use the practice tools?
Choose one target skill, complete a session, record difficult question types and return to those areas in a later session. Use the study plan to keep the next action visible.
