Why we exist

Over 1.2 million Indian students pursue degrees abroad, and families spend ₹40–60 lakhs on a single postgraduate degree. Yet the journey is fragmented: one app for IELTS practice, one consultant for admissions, another agent for visas — and nothing connects them.

The Foreign Express connects them. Your AI IELTS coach doesn't stop at a score — it converts your performance into a university shortlist, a scholarship estimate, and a live counsellor handoff inside the same dashboard.

Preparation feeds admission. Admission feeds visa. Visa feeds your career. One account. One data trail. One team accountable end to end.
